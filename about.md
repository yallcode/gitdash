# GitDash

Ever spend an hour on a section, accidentally delete half of it, and realize undo only goes back so far? That's what this mod is for.

GitDash saves a copy of your level every time you hit save. When something goes wrong, you open the Timeline from the editor pause menu and pick which save to go back to. It takes like two taps.

The main difference from undo/redo is that undo gets cleared when you close the editor. GitDash keeps your save history around no matter what.

---

**How to use it:**

Save your level like normal. When you need to roll back, open the pause menu → tap Timeline → use the arrows to find the save you want → tap Restore.

---

**Settings you can change:**

- How many snapshots to keep per level (default is 50, can go up to 200)
- Whether to also snapshot on autosave (off by default)
- Whether to show exact timestamps or just "5 min ago"
